# Shinobi Cash

Cross-chain privacy protocol powered by Privacy Pools and zero-knowledge proofs.

## What is Shinobi Cash?

Shinobi Cash is a **cross-chain privacy protocol** that enables private transactions across the Ethereum ecosystem. Deposit from any chain, withdraw to any chain — all while maintaining privacy through a unified pool.

**Key Features:**
- **Cross-Chain Privacy** — Deposit on Base, withdraw on Arbitrum (or any supported chain)
- **Unified Anonymity Set** — One pool across all chains means stronger privacy
- **Compliant by Design** — Prove your funds came from a compliant source without revealing which deposit is yours
- **No Gas Required** — Withdraw without ETH; gas is paid from your withdrawal via ERC-4337

## How It Works

### For Users

1. **Connect Wallet** — Sign in with your wallet to derive your private keys
2. **Deposit** — Send ETH to the privacy pool from any supported chain
3. **Wait for Activation** — Your deposit enters the anonymity set
4. **Withdraw** — Withdraw to any address on any supported chain using a zero-knowledge proof

### The Technology

Shinobi Cash combines several cutting-edge technologies:

- **Privacy Pools** — Merkle tree commitments and nullifiers for privacy
- **Zero-Knowledge Proofs** — Groth16 SNARKs prove withdrawal validity without revealing the deposit
- **Open Intent Framework (OIF)** — Cross-chain settlement via solvers and oracles
- **Association Set Providers (ASPs)** — Enable compliance without surveillance

## Why Shinobi Cash?

### Unified Pool = Stronger Privacy

Traditional privacy solutions fragment users across chains. Each chain has its own small pool, weakening the anonymity set.

Shinobi Cash treats the **Ethereum ecosystem as a single privacy domain**. One pool. Every chain. Maximum anonymity.

### Compliant, Not Shady

Privacy doesn't mean hiding from regulators. With ASPs, you can prove your funds came from a compliant source — without revealing *which* deposit is yours.

### Simple User Experience

- No complex bridging — deposit and withdraw seamlessly across chains
- No wallet gas needed — relay fee deducted from withdrawal amount
- One unified interface — manage all your privacy across chains
